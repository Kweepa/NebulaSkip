The pistol is the only weapon. There is no weapon selection and no weapon keys.

It overlays the **bottom of the 3D viewport** (viewport is at the bottom of the screen; HUD is above) pretending to be line draws, but it is a rectangle stamped into the mode-E bitmap.
Muzzle flash is a second rectangle masquerading as a line draw. In jump mode the crosshair is stamped in its place.
